This module lets each company control which of its contracts are picked
up by the recurring invoices cron.

Per company you can:

- Disable recurring invoice generation entirely.
- Restrict generation to the contracts matching an extra domain, on top
  of the domains contributed by the standard flow and other contract
  modules.

Configuration
=============

To configure this module:

1. Go to *Settings > Contracts*.
2. Use *Create recurring invoices* to enable or disable recurring
   invoice generation for the current company (enabled by default).
3. When enabled, set an optional domain to restrict which contracts of
   the company are invoiced by the cron.

The setting is per company, so switch the active company to configure
another one.

Usage
=====

The configured behaviour applies automatically to the *Generate
Recurring Invoices from Contracts* cron and to any code relying on
``_get_contracts_to_invoice_domain``:

- *Create recurring invoices* unset: no contract of the company is
  invoiced.
- *Create recurring invoices* set, no domain: standard behaviour,
  nothing is added.
- *Create recurring invoices* set, with a domain: the domain is applied
  (with AND) to the contracts of the company only.

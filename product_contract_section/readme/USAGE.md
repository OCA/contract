- Create a sale order with contract products and organise its lines with
  sections and notes.
- Confirm the order (or use *Create Contract*) to generate the contract(s).
- The generated contract(s) reproduce the order's sections and notes:

  - a section appears on the contract of each product it spans (up to the next
    section), so a section shared by several contracts is repeated in each,
  - a note appears on the contract of the product line above it.

- When upselling or downselling an existing contract from a new sale order:

  - a section the contract already has is not duplicated,
  - a new section present on the order is added to the contract.

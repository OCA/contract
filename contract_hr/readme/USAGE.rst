#. Go to *Human Resources > Employees*.
#. Create or edit an employee with type "Freelance" or "Contractor".
#. In the employee form, you will see a "Contractor/Freelancer" button in the button box.
#. Click on the "Contractor/Freelancer" button to view/create contracts for this employee.
#. Alternatively, go to *Human Resources > Payroll > Contractor/Freelancer* to manage all freelancer contracts.
#. Create a new contract and select the employee (only freelance/contractor employees will be available).
#. The employee's department and job position will be automatically filled from the employee record.
#. Fill in the contract details including partner, products, and payment terms.
#. The contract will be linked to the employee and can be used for invoicing and contract management.

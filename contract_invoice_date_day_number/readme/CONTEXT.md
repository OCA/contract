- Accountant users need to generate invoices a certain amount of time before invoice 
  validation and final date in order to review them. Or under some agreements with
  customers, they need to get invoice date to be on a particular date.
  In order to do so, we introduce a day number to be used for the invoice date.
  e.g.: The invoices are generated every month on the first day with an invoice date
  on the 10th of that month.

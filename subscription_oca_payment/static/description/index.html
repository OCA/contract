Subscription Payments

This module extends Subscription management (subscription_oca) so subscriptions can charge a customer’s saved payment method (a payment token) automatically on each billing run, so no manual collection step is needed. This is intended for recurring merchant-initiated charges (for example SEPA direct debit or a stored card via a tokenising payment provider).

The defining principle is charge before posting: the invoice is kept in draft and is only posted (and reconciled) once the payment succeeds. A failed charge therefore never leaves a posted invoice owed by the customer and never consumes an invoice number.

Configuration

  1. Configure a payment provider that supports tokenisation and set its Payment Journal. Charges cannot be made through a provider without a journal.
  2. Go to Subscriptions > Configuration > Subscription templates and open or create a template.
  3. Pick an Invoicing mode (see the table below for how each one behaves once automatic payment is on).
  4. Tick Automatic payment.
  5. Optionally, set the settings that appear below it:
    • Payment Failure Email: emailed to the customer when an automatic payment fails. Empty by default; the Subscription: Automatic Payment Failure template is provided. Leave it empty if the payment provider already notifies the customer (GoCardless does).
    • Retry failed payments: unticked by default. When ticked, a failed payment is charged again automatically, up to Payment retries times (default 3), Hours between retries apart (default 24). Leave it unticked if the payment provider retries failed payments itself (for example GoCardless), otherwise the customer could be charged twice.

Optionally, a default payment token can be set on the customer, in the Sales & Purchase tab of the contact (Subscription Payment Token). It is charged for subscriptions with automatic payment that have no Payment Token of their own.

Automatic payment is orthogonal to the invoicing mode and works with all of them, including Draft.

Invoicing mode On a successful charge
Draft Invoice posted, no email (silent background billing)
Invoice Invoice posted, paid invoice emailed
Invoice & send Invoice posted, paid invoice emailed
Sale order & Invoice Sale order confirmed, invoice posted (no email)

The paid invoice is emailed once by the subscription, also when the Automatic Invoice setting of Sales is enabled.

Usage

Assigning the payment token

On a subscription whose template has Automatic payment enabled, a Payment Token field appears. It can be set in three ways:

  • Manually - pick any saved token belonging to the customer.
  • Suggested automatically - when you select the partner or the template, the customer’s Subscription Payment Token is proposed, or else the most recent token saved for the customer, its company or the company’s other contacts (in the subscription’s company). A token you set manually is never silently overwritten.
  • Carried over from a sale - see Onboarding from eCommerce below.

If the field is left empty, the customer’s Subscription Payment Token is charged.

A payment token that has been removed (archived), for example by the customer from their portal, is never charged; the customer’s Subscription Payment Token is used instead if set, otherwise the subscription is flagged with Payment Exception.

A token belonging to a different commercial partner cannot be assigned; this is enforced by a constraint.

What happens on each billing run

When the Subscriptions Management cron job generates an invoice for a subscription with Automatic payment:

  1. A draft invoice is created for the period (or the period’s draft left over from a failed attempt is reused, so retries never pile up duplicates).
  2. An offline payment transaction is created against the saved token and submitted to the provider.
  3. On success the invoice is posted, reconciled with the payment, and - depending on the invoicing mode - emailed to the customer as a paid document. The customer never receives an “amount due” document for money already taken. The next invoice date is then moved on.
  4. On asynchronous capture (e.g. direct debit) the transaction is left pending and the invoice stays draft; it is posted (and emailed, as above) when the provider confirms the charge, and only then is the next invoice date moved on. Until then the subscription is not invoiced again.
  5. On failure the invoice stays draft, the subscription is flagged (see Payment failures) and the next invoice date is not advanced, so the same period is retried once the issue is fixed.

The Create Invoice button on a subscription does not charge the token: it creates a draft invoice and advances the next invoice date, as it does without automatic payment.

Use cases

  • Stored-card billing (synchronous) - the charge is captured immediately; the invoice is posted, reconciled and emailed in the same run.
  • Direct debit / asynchronous capture - the charge is submitted and the provider confirms it later via webhook; the invoice is posted and emailed on confirmation.
  • Silent background billing - use Draft mode with Automatic payment to collect and post without ever emailing the customer.
  • Onboarding from a webshop sale - see below.

Payment failures

If a charge cannot be collected (no token, a provider without a journal, the request could not be sent, or the provider rejects it), or the provider later reports that an accepted payment failed or was cancelled (for example a failed direct debit), the subscription is:

  • flagged with Payment Exception, showing the Failed Payment, with a chatter note giving the provider’s reason,
  • emailed, if a Payment Failure Email is set on the template (not for configuration errors such as a provider without a journal),
  • given a to-do activity (visible in the list and kanban views) so a salesperson is alerted, and
  • left with an unpaid invoice: a draft one, or a posted one whose payment was cancelled.

While the flag is set, the cron job skips the subscription, so a broken payment method does not generate repeated invoices or charges. Use the Payment exception filter to list them.

Once the payment method has been fixed, either:

  • click Retry payment to charge the unpaid invoices again, oldest first. The flag and the activity are cleared as soon as the provider accepts the payment (a pending direct debit counts). This also works after the automatic retries below have been used up, or
  • clear Payment Exception to resume invoicing; the current period’s draft is then charged on the next run. Clearing the flag also resolves the activity.

If Retry failed payments is ticked on the template, a scheduled action also charges failed invoices again automatically, up to the configured number of retries. After the last retry the flag stays set for a salesperson to follow up.

If the provider retries the payment itself (for example a direct-debit resubmission) and it succeeds, the flag is cleared automatically. A provider module that reports a payment it is retrying as pending doesn’t flag the subscription at all. If yours reports it as failed while still retrying, don’t click Retry payment or clear Payment Exception: the customer could be charged twice. Wait until the provider reports that it has stopped retrying.

Chargebacks don’t change the payment’s state, so they only flag the subscription if the payment provider module reports them by calling payment.transaction._subscription_payment_reversed(). Otherwise, handle them in the payment provider. The invoice stays paid, so there is nothing to retry: follow up the reinstated debt, and clear Payment Exception manually if the subscription was flagged.

Onboarding from eCommerce

A customer’s first token is typically captured when they buy a subscription product online and pay with a tokenising provider. When the sale order is confirmed:

  1. A subscription is created from the order’s subscribable products (via their Subscription template), as usual.
  2. If that template has Automatic payment enabled, the token saved during the order’s online payment is copied onto the new subscription automatically.

From the next billing cycle onward the subscription charges that token without any manual setup.

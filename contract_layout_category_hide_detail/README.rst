- Brings the section hide-detail options of
  *sale_layout_category_hide_detail* to contracts.

- On each contract section you can:

  - hide the section's line details,
  - show or hide the section subtotal,
  - hide the individual line amounts.

- These options drive:

  - what is printed on the contract report,
  - what is shown on the customer portal,
  - the sections and settings carried over to the invoices generated
    from the contract.

- When a contract is created from a sale order (through
  *product_contract* and *product_contract_section*), the section
  settings defined on the quotation are propagated to the contract.

Usage
=====

- Open a contract and add a section line to its recurring items.

- On the section row, use the icons to toggle:

  - *Details*: hide or show the lines that belong to the section,
  - *Section subtotal*: when the details are hidden, show or hide the
    section subtotal,
  - *Subtotal*: when the details are shown, show or hide the section
    subtotal,
  - *Line amount*: hide or show the unit price and subtotal of each
    line.

- Check the result on:

  - the contract PDF report,
  - the contract in the customer portal,
  - the recurring invoices generated from the contract (sections and
    settings are copied over).

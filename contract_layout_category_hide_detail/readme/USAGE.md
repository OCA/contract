- Open a contract and add a section line to its recurring items.
- On the section row, use the icons to toggle:

  - *Details*: hide or show the lines that belong to the section,
  - *Section subtotal*: when the details are hidden, show or hide the section
    subtotal,
  - *Subtotal*: when the details are shown, show or hide the section subtotal,
  - *Line amount*: hide or show the unit price and subtotal of each line.

- Check the result on:

  - the contract PDF report,
  - the contract in the customer portal,
  - the recurring invoices generated from the contract (sections and settings
    are copied over).

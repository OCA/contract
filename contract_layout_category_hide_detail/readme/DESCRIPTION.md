- Brings the section hide-detail options of *sale_layout_category_hide_detail*
  to contracts.
- On each contract section you can:

  - hide the section's line details,
  - show or hide the section subtotal,
  - hide the individual line amounts.

- These options drive:

  - what is printed on the contract report,
  - what is shown on the customer portal,
  - the sections and settings carried over to the invoices generated from the
    contract.

- When a contract is created from a sale order (through *product_contract* and
  *product_contract_section*), the section settings defined on the quotation
  are propagated to the contract.

## Viewing Contract Dates

1. Navigate to **Contacts** → **Contacts**
2. Open any partner record
3. Click on the **Contracts** tab to view:
   - **First Contract Date**: The earliest contract start date
   - **Contract Anniversary Date**: This year's anniversary date (automatically calculated)

## Searching by Anniversary Date

1. Go to **Contacts** → **Contacts**
2. Click the **Filters** button
3. Select **Advanced Search**
4. Use the **Contract Anniversary Date** field to:
   - Find contacts with anniversaries on specific dates
   - Filter for upcoming anniversaries
   - Identify contacts without contracts

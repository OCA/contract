1. On a contract product, in the **Contract** tab, choose **Minimum Term** as
   contract duration and set the minimum term. The renewal (**Auto Renew**)
   and the termination notice stay where they are, they now apply to the
   minimum term.
2. Sell the product: the sale order line (and the contract configurator) shows
   the minimum term instead of the duration.
3. When the sale order is confirmed, the contract line is created without end
   date and with the minimum term of the sale order line.
